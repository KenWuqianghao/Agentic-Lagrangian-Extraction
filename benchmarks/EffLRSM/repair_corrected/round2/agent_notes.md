**Summary of the change made to `model.fr`:**

I found one construction in the Lagrangian that stands out from an otherwise standard set of currents: in `LZRLeptons`, the heavy Majorana neutrino field `HN` was coupled to itself (same flavor index `ii` on both sides) through the Z_R current using a single chirality projector:

`bar[HN[ii]].Ga[mu].gZRNR ProjP.HN[ii]`

For a self-conjugate (Majorana) field, the plain vector current `bar[N].Ga[mu].N` vanishes identically, so this term is mathematically exactly equal to half the axial current: `ProjP = (1+Ga[5])/2`, and the vector piece drops out, leaving `(gZRNR/2) bar[HN].Ga[mu].Ga[5].HN`. Writing a same-flavor Majorana neutral current with a bare chirality projector (rather than the manifestly well-formed axial form) is a known trouble spot for FeynRules' Majorana Feynman-rule derivation, and it is the only non-standard bilinear structure in the file — every other current pairs `HN` with a distinct field (`l`, `ve`), which is unproblematic. This single term is the most plausible trigger for the cascading corruption seen in the report: parallel-kernel warnings (`Join::heads`, `Part::partw`, `Part::pkspec1`) during the consistency-check phase, followed by garbled `PartNameMG`/`ConvertSpinToString[0]` output contaminating unrelated, purely-SM flavor-indexed vertices later in the same session — the same failure signature (widespread corruption of vertices unrelated to the edited term) that Attempt 1 diagnosed correctly in kind, just for a different symbol.

I replaced the term with its exact equivalent, `(gZRNR/2) bar[HN[ii]].Ga[mu].Ga[5].HN[ii]`, which preserves the identical physical coupling strength and flavor-diagonal structure while using the standard, robust axial-current form for a self-paired Majorana bilinear. No field content, quantum numbers, other operator structures, or parameter values were changed.

One caveat for the next round: the `mass_diagonal`/`mass_spectrum` failures cite the WR-quark charged-current term (`gWRq·CKMR[i,j]·bar[u].Ga[mu].ProjP.d·WR[mu]`) directly and fairly cleanly, which is consistent with either (a) being a corrupted echo of the same crash (my leading hypothesis, since there is no genuine scalar mass-mixing term anywhere in the file — `CKMR`, `YR`, `XL` are used exclusively inside vector-current terms, never as bare mass insertions), or (b) a separate limitation in how FeynRules numerically resolves the `External` flavor matrices `CKMR`/`YR`/`XL` during the consistency check. If the checks still fail after this fix, that check's handling of those three matrices is the next place to look — I did not alter them here since their `Value` declarations (diagonal defaults) already match the standard FeynRules convention and changing them further would have required either guessing at internal FeynRules check semantics or reducing the matrices' external tunability, which would go beyond a purely technical fix.
