# Reconstruction of `sanitized.fr`

The file adds a spontaneously broken abelian gauge group to the Standard Model.
The new group is a one-parameter family of $U(1)$ charges. The file also adds one
real scalar, one heavy neutral gauge boson, and three Majorana singlet fermions.

Notation used below:

* $P_{R}=\tfrac{1}{2}(1+\gamma_{5})=\texttt{ProjP}$, $P_{L}=\tfrac{1}{2}(1-\gamma_{5})=\texttt{ProjM}$.
* $\Phi$ is the SM Higgs doublet (`Phi`), $\Phi^{\dagger}$ is `Phibar`, $v$ is `vev`.
* $\epsilon_{ij}$ is `Eps[ii,jj]` with $\epsilon_{12}=+1$; all $SU(2)_{L}$ indices are written down.
* $i,j$ are $SU(2)_{L}$ indices; $a,b$ are generation indices.
* Repeated generation indices are summed.

---

## 1. Lagrangian

The total Lagrangian is `LTotal`:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}
=\mathcal{L}_{\rm SM}
+\mathcal{L}_{Z'{\rm kin}}
+\mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
+\mathcal{L}_{N{\rm kin}}
+\mathcal{L}_{H_{X}{\rm kin}}
+\mathcal{L}_{Z'H_{X}}
+\mathcal{L}_{N{\rm Yuk}}
+\mathcal{L}_{\nu{\rm Yuk}}
+\mathcal{L}_{V_{X}} .
$$

### 1.0 Gauge content of the covariant derivative

The file never puts the new gauge boson inside a `DC` covariant derivative. The
SM covariant derivative from `LSM` is unchanged:

$$
D_{\mu}\psi=\Big(\partial_{\mu}-ig_{s}T^{A}G^{A}_{\mu}-ig\,T^{I}W^{I}_{\mu}-ig'Y_{\psi}B_{\mu}\Big)\psi .
$$

All $Z'$ couplings are written by hand in `LZpF` and `LZpHX`. The scalar term
`LZpHX` and the mass `MZp` are exactly what

$$
D_{\mu}\Phi_{X}=\Big(\partial_{\mu}+i\,g_{X}x_{\Phi_{X}}Z'_{\mu}\Big)\Phi_{X},
\qquad
\Phi_{X}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(v_{\Phi}+H_{X}\big),
\qquad x_{\Phi_{X}}\equiv x_{Sc}=2x_{\Phi},
$$

produces from $|D_{\mu}\Phi_{X}|^{2}$. So the intended charge normalisation is

$$
D_{\mu}f=\Big(\partial_{\mu}+\dots+i\,g_{X}x_{f}Z'_{\mu}\Big)f ,
$$

with the $U(1)_{X}$ charge of every field fixed by the two input numbers
$x_{H}$ and $x_{\Phi}$:

$$
x_{f}=x_{H}\,Y_{f}+x_{\Phi}\,(B-L)_{f}.
$$

The fermion current term `LZpF` in this file carries an **extra overall factor
of 2** relative to that covariant derivative (see Section 5, Note 1).

There is **no** gauge kinetic mixing term $-\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}B_{\mu\nu}Z'^{\mu\nu}$
and **no** explicit $Z'$ coupling to the SM Higgs doublet in the file.

### 1.1 `LZpKin` — $Z'$ kinetic and mass term

`FS[Zp,mu,nu]` is the abelian field strength $Z'_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}Z'_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}Z'_{\mu}$.

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'{\rm kin}}
=-\frac{1}{4}\,Z'_{\mu\nu}Z'^{\mu\nu}
+\frac{1}{2}\,M_{Z'}^{2}\,Z'_{\mu}Z'^{\mu}.
$$

The mass term is written explicitly (Proca form) instead of being generated by
the scalar kinetic terms.

### 1.2 `LZpF` — $Z'$ neutral current

Written with the doublets kept whole:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
=-2\,g_{X}\,Z'_{\mu}\Big[
 x_{q_{L}}\,\bar{Q}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}Q_{L}
+x_{u_{R}}\,\bar{u}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}u_{R}
+x_{d_{R}}\,\bar{d}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}d_{R}
+x_{\ell_{L}}\,\bar{L}_{L}\gamma^{\mu}L_{L}
+x_{e_{R}}\,\bar{e}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}e_{R}
+x_{n_{R}}\,\bar{N}_{R}\gamma^{\mu}N_{R}\Big].
$$

With the chiral projectors made explicit on four-component fields, and with
`NR` $=P_{R}N_{X}$ substituted:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
=-2\,g_{X}\,Z'_{\mu}\sum_{a}\Big[
 x_{q_{L}}\big(\bar{u}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}u_{a}+\bar{d}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}d_{a}\big)
+x_{u_{R}}\,\bar{u}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}u_{a}
+x_{d_{R}}\,\bar{d}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}d_{a}
$$
$$
\qquad\qquad
+x_{\ell_{L}}\big(\bar{\nu}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\nu_{a}+\bar{e}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}e_{a}\big)
+x_{e_{R}}\,\bar{e}_{a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}e_{a}
+x_{n_{R}}\,\bar{N}_{X,a}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}N_{X,a}\Big].
$$

Points of detail:

* The overall factor is $2\times(-g_{X})=-2g_{X}$. The `.fr` source multiplies the
  whole bracket by an explicit `2`.
* `ExpandIndices[..., FlavorExpand -> {SU2D}]` splits $Q_{L}\to(u_{L},d_{L})$ and
  $L_{L}\to(\nu_{L},e_{L})$.
* The replacement rules
  $V_{ab}V^{*}_{ac}\to\delta_{bc}$ and $V_{ba}V^{*}_{ca}\to\delta_{bc}$
  use CKM unitarity. They remove the CKM matrices that the doublet expansion
  introduces, so the $Z'$ current is flavour diagonal. There is no
  flavour-changing neutral current at tree level.
* The current is vector-like in colour and flavour, but **chiral**: left-handed
  and right-handed charges differ whenever $x_{H}\neq 0$.
* $N_{R}$ is purely right-chiral, so only $P_{R}$ appears for the singlet fermion.

### 1.3 `LNXKin` — Majorana kinetic and mass term

$$
\mathcal{L}_{N{\rm kin}}
=\frac{i}{2}\,\bar{N}_{X,a}\gamma^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}N_{X,a}
-\frac{1}{2}\,\bar{N}_{X,a}\,(m_{N})_{ab}\,N_{X,b},
\qquad (m_{N})_{ab}={\rm diag}\big(M_{N_{1}},M_{N_{2}},M_{N_{3}}\big).
$$

The factors $\tfrac{1}{2}$ are the standard Majorana normalisation, because
$N_{X}$ is declared `SelfConjugate -> True` ($N_{X}^{c}=N_{X}$). The mass matrix
is diagonal in generation space, so this is a lepton-number violating
($\Delta L=2$) Majorana mass.

### 1.4 `LHXKin` — real scalar kinetic and mass term

$$
\mathcal{L}_{H_{X}{\rm kin}}
=\frac{1}{2}\,\partial_{\mu}H_{X}\,\partial^{\mu}H_{X}
-\frac{1}{2}\,M_{H_{X}}^{2}\,H_{X}^{2}.
$$

### 1.5 `LZpHX` — $Z'$–scalar gauge interactions

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'H_{X}}
=g_{X}^{2}\,x_{Sc}^{2}\Big(v_{\Phi}H_{X}+\frac{1}{2}H_{X}^{2}\Big)Z'_{\mu}Z'^{\mu}.
$$

These are the cross terms and the quadratic term of
$|D_{\mu}\Phi_{X}|^{2}$ with $\Phi_{X}=(v_{\Phi}+H_{X})/\sqrt{2}$. The constant
piece $\tfrac{1}{2}g_{X}^{2}x_{Sc}^{2}v_{\Phi}^{2}Z'_{\mu}Z'^{\mu}$ is not written
here; it is inside `MZp`.

### 1.6 `LNYuk` — Majorana Yukawa coupling

$$
\mathcal{L}_{N{\rm Yuk}}
=-\frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\,H_{X}\,\bar{N}_{X,a}\,(y_{N})_{ab}\,N_{X,b},
\qquad (y_{N})_{ab}={\rm diag}\Big(\tfrac{\sqrt{2}M_{N_{1}}}{v_{\Phi}},
\tfrac{\sqrt{2}M_{N_{2}}}{v_{\Phi}},\tfrac{\sqrt{2}M_{N_{3}}}{v_{\Phi}}\Big).
$$

This is the fluctuation part of $-\tfrac{y_{N}}{2\sqrt{2}}\Phi_{X}\bar{N}_{X}N_{X}$
after $\Phi_{X}\to v_{\Phi}+H_{X}$. The constant part reproduces `mNmaj`:
$M_{N_{a}}=y_{N,aa}v_{\Phi}/\sqrt{2}$. The scalar coupling is therefore not free;
it is fixed by $M_{N_{a}}$ and $v_{\Phi}$.

### 1.7 `LNuYuk` — Dirac neutrino Yukawa coupling

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu{\rm Yuk}}
=-(y_{\nu})_{ab}\;\epsilon_{ij}\;
\bar{L}^{\,i}_{L,a}\,\Phi^{\dagger\,j}\,P_{R}N_{X,b}
\;+\;{\rm h.c.}
$$

Equivalently, with $\tilde{\Phi}^{i}\equiv\epsilon_{ij}\Phi^{\dagger j}$
(that is $\tilde{\Phi}=i\sigma_{2}\Phi^{*}$):

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu{\rm Yuk}}
=-(y_{\nu})_{ab}\,\bar{L}_{L,a}\,\tilde{\Phi}\,P_{R}N_{X,b}
-(y_{\nu})^{*}_{ab}\,\bar{N}_{X,b}\,P_{L}\,\tilde{\Phi}^{\dagger}L_{L,a}.
$$

Component form with $\Phi=(\phi^{+},\phi^{0})^{T}$ and $\epsilon_{12}=+1$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu{\rm Yuk}}
=-(y_{\nu})_{ab}\Big[\bar{\nu}_{L,a}\,\phi^{0\ast}-\bar{e}_{L,a}\,\phi^{-}\Big]P_{R}N_{X,b}+{\rm h.c.}
$$

Points of detail:

* `NR[sp,ff] :> ProjP[sp,sp2] NX[sp2,ff]` means $N_{R}=P_{R}N_{X}$. The chirality
  structure is $\bar{L}_{L}\,(\cdots)\,P_{R}N_{X}$, that is left-handed to
  right-handed.
* `HC[yuk]` supplies the hermitian conjugate with $(y_{\nu})^{*}$ and $P_{L}$.
* After electroweak breaking, $\phi^{0}\to(v+h+iG^{0})/\sqrt{2}$, this gives a
  Dirac mass $m_{D}=y_{\nu}v/\sqrt{2}$ between $\nu_{L}$ and $N_{X}$.
* `feynmangaugerules` sets the Goldstone fields $G^{0},G^{\pm}$ to zero when
  `FeynmanGauge` is `False` (unitary gauge).

### 1.8 `LPotX` — scalar potential terms

$$
\mathcal{L}_{V_{X}}
=-\lambda_{\Phi}\,v_{\Phi}\,H_{X}^{3}
-\frac{\lambda_{\Phi}}{4}\,H_{X}^{4}
-\frac{\lambda'}{2}\,H_{X}^{2}\,\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi .
$$

The cubic and quartic self-couplings come from expanding
$\lambda_{\Phi}\big(|\Phi_{X}|^{2}-v_{\Phi}^{2}/2\big)^{2}$; the quadratic piece is
already in `MHX`, and $M_{H_{X}}^{2}=2\lambda_{\Phi}v_{\Phi}^{2}$. The last term is
the Higgs portal between the new singlet and the SM doublet.

### 3.2 Internal parameters

All charges follow one rule, $x_{f}=x_{H}Y_{f}+x_{\Phi}(B-L)_{f}$:

| Symbol | Formula | Value at $x_{H}=2,\;x_{\Phi}=1$ | Charge of |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xqL` | $x_{H}/6+x_{\Phi}/3$ | $2/3$ | quark doublet $Q_{L}$ |
| `xuR` | $2x_{H}/3+x_{\Phi}/3$ | $5/3$ | up-type singlet $u_{R}$ |
| `xdR` | $-x_{H}/3+x_{\Phi}/3$ | $-1/3$ | down-type singlet $d_{R}$ |
| `xlL` | $-x_{H}/2-x_{\Phi}$ | $-2$ | lepton doublet $L_{L}$ |
| `xeR` | $-x_{H}-x_{\Phi}$ | $-3$ | charged lepton singlet $e_{R}$ |
| `xnR` | $-x_{\Phi}$ | $-1$ | singlet neutrino $N_{R}$ |
| `xHd` | $x_{H}/2$ | $1$ | SM Higgs doublet $\Phi$ |
| `xSc` | $2x_{\Phi}$ | $2$ | breaking scalar $\Phi_{X}$ |

These charges cancel all gauge and gravitational anomalies for any $x_{H}$ and
$x_{\Phi}$, because they are a linear combination of hypercharge and $B-L$ with
three right-handed neutrinos. They also make both Yukawa terms gauge invariant:

* $\bar{L}_{L}\tilde{\Phi}N_{R}$: $-x_{\ell_{L}}-x_{Hd}+x_{n_{R}}=2-1-1=0$.
* $\Phi_{X}\bar{N}_{X}N_{X}$: $x_{Sc}+2x_{n_{R}}=2-2=0$.

|---|---|---|---|
| `MZp` | $g_{X}\sqrt{x_{Sc}^{2}v_{\Phi}^{2}+x_{Hd}^{2}v^{2}}$ | $\approx 7500.6$ GeV | $Z'$ mass, dominated by $v_{\Phi}$; the $v$ piece is the small electroweak contribution |
| `MHX` | $\sqrt{2\lambda_{\Phi}v_{\Phi}^{2}}$ | $\approx 4192.6$ GeV | mass of the singlet scalar |

## 4. Physics summary

This is a general anomaly-free $U(1)_{X}$ extension of the Standard Model, where
the charge of each field is $x_{H}Y+x_{\Phi}(B-L)$; setting $x_{H}=0$ gives pure
$B-L$, and $x_{H}\to\infty$ gives pure hypercharge. The new states are a heavy
neutral gauge boson $Z'$ ($\approx 7.5$ TeV), a real singlet scalar $H_{X}$
($\approx 4.2$ TeV) from the $U(1)_{X}$ breaking field, and three Majorana
right-handed neutrinos $N_{X,1,2,3}$ ($8$ TeV each) whose masses come from the
same VEV $v_{\Phi}$. A small Dirac Yukawa $Y_{\nu}\sim10^{-6}$ plus the large
Majorana mass gives light neutrino masses by the type-I seesaw.

The model mediates Drell-Yan production $q\bar{q}\to Z'\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-},
q\bar{q},\nu\bar{\nu}$ with generation-universal and flavour-conserving but
chiral couplings, plus $Z'\to N_{X}N_{X}$ and $Z'\to Z H_{X}$-type decays when
kinematics allow. Pair produced heavy neutrinos decay through the Dirac Yukawa
to $\ell W$, $\nu Z$ and $\nu h$, giving same-sign dilepton and lepton-number
violating final states. The singlet scalar $H_{X}$ connects to the visible
sector through the portal coupling $\lambda'$ and through $H_{X}Z'Z'$ and
$H_{X}N_{X}N_{X}$, so it appears as a heavy Higgs-like resonance.

## 5. Notes a checker should look at

1. **Factor of 2 in `LZpF`.** The fermion current block is multiplied by an
   explicit `2`, giving $-2g_{X}x_{f}Z'_{\mu}\bar{f}\gamma^{\mu}f$. The scalar
   sector (`LZpHX`) and the mass formula (`MZp`) use the unmultiplied
   normalisation $g_{X}x_{Sc}$. The two sectors are therefore inconsistent: all
   $Z'$-fermion vertices are twice the size implied by the covariant derivative
   that fixes $M_{Z'}$, so the $Z'$ partial widths into fermions are four times
   larger than the charge assignment implies. Read as a physical model, this
   factor should not be there.
2. **No $Z'$ coupling to the SM Higgs doublet.** `MZp` contains an
   $x_{Hd}^{2}v^{2}$ term, which assumes $\Phi$ carries $U(1)_{X}$ charge
   $x_{Hd}$. But no $Z'$–$\Phi$ interaction and no $Z$–$Z'$ mass mixing term is
   written, so the electroweak part of the $Z'$ mass has no matching vertices.
3. **No gauge kinetic mixing** ($B_{\mu\nu}Z'^{\mu\nu}$) is present; the model
   assumes it is zero at the input scale.
4. **No $H_{X}$–$h$ mixing term** of the form $\lambda'v_{\Phi}H_{X}\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi$
   appears in `LPotX`; only the quadratic portal $H_{X}^{2}\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi$ is
   present, so $H_{X}$ and the SM Higgs do not mix at tree level in this file.
5. `Width` for $Z'$ is fixed at $1353$ GeV, about $18\%$ of $M_{Z'}$. That is a
   hard-coded number, not a computed width.
