# Reconstruction of `sanitized.fr`

## 0. Scope and conventions

The file declares **two new gauge bosons** (one charged, one neutral) and **three
heavy Majorana neutrinos**, and gives *only* their interaction terms with the
Standard Model fermions.

Conventions used below, following the FeynRules idioms present in the file:

| `.fr` idiom | Standard notation |
| --- | --- |
| `Ga[mu]` | $\gamma^\mu$ |
| `bar[psi]` | $\bar\psi$ |
| `ProjP` | $P_R=\tfrac{1}{2}(1+\gamma_5)$ |
| `ProjM` | $P_L=\tfrac{1}{2}(1-\gamma_5)$ |
| `HC[X]` | $X^\dagger$ (hermitian conjugate) |
| `u[i,c]`, `d[i,c]` | SM up/down quark, generation $i$, colour $c$ |
| `l[i]`, `ve[i]` | SM charged lepton and light neutrino, generation $i$ |
| `WR[mu]` | $W^{+}_{R\,\mu}$ (the field carries $Q=+1$, so it annihilates $W_R^+$) |
| `ZR[mu]` | $Z_{R\,\mu}$ (real field) |
| `N[n]` | heavy Majorana neutrino $N_n$ |

The file uses `ee`, `sw`, `cw` ($e$, $s_W$, $c_W$) and the fields `u`, `d`, `l`,
`ve`. These are **not declared here**, so this file is a fragment that must be
loaded on top of a Standard Model FeynRules implementation.

### Covariant derivative and gauge content

**No `DC[...]` covariant derivative and no `FS[...]` field strength appear
anywhere in the file.** There are therefore:

* no kinetic terms for $W_R$, $Z_R$ or $N$;
* no mass terms (masses are declared numerically in the particle classes);
* no scalar sector and no $W_L$–$W_R$ or $Z$–$Z_R$ mixing terms;
* no triple or quartic gauge self-couplings ($W_R W_R \gamma$, $W_R W_R Z$, ...).

The new vectors enter **only** through explicit current $\times$ field products.
This is the defining feature of an *effective* (simplified) construction: the
underlying gauge structure is not written out, it is encoded in the numerical
values of the coupling constants.

The gauge content can still be read off from those values. Writing

$$g_R \;\equiv\; \kappa_R\,\frac{e}{s_W}\,,\qquad
x \;\equiv\; \left(\frac{s_W}{c_W\,\kappa_R}\right)^{2}
\;=\;\left(\frac{g_Y}{g_R}\right)^{2},\qquad g_Y=\frac{e}{c_W},$$

every chiral $Z_R$ charge in the file satisfies

$$g^{f}_{L,R} \;=\; (1-x)\,T^{f}_{3R} \;-\; x\,\frac{(B-L)_f}{2}\,,$$

with $T_{3R}=+\tfrac12$ for $u_R,N_R$, $-\tfrac12$ for $d_R,e_R$, $0$ for all
left-handed fields, and $(B-L)=+\tfrac13$ for quarks, $-1$ for leptons. This is
the current of the neutral generator of
$SU(2)_R\times U(1)_{B-L}$ that is orthogonal to SM hypercharge, i.e. the
covariant derivative that is *implied* (but not written) is

$$D_\mu \supset i\,g_R\,T^a_R W^{a}_{R\,\mu} \;+\; i\,g_{B-L}\,\frac{B-L}{2}\,B^{B-L}_{\mu},$$

with $W_R^{\pm}=(W_R^1\mp iW_R^2)/\sqrt2$ and $Z_R$ the combination of $W_R^3$
and $B^{B-L}$ orthogonal to the SM $B_\mu$. The overall factors $g_{W_Rq}$,
$g_{W_R\ell}$, $g_{Z_Rq}$, $g_{Z_R\ell}$ carry independent quark and lepton
normalisations ($\kappa_R^q \ne \kappa_R^\ell$ is allowed), which is *not*
possible in a genuine gauge theory — another sign that this is an effective
parametrisation.

## 1. Lagrangian

### 1.1 Charged current: $W_R$ with quarks

`LWRQuarksNonHC`

$$\mathcal{L}_{W_Rq}^{\rm noHC}
= -\,g_{W_R q}\sum_{i=1}^{3}\sum_{j=1}^{3}\sum_{c=1}^{3}
V^{\prime}_{ij}\;
\bar u_{i}^{\,c}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,P_R\,d_{j}^{\,c}\;W^{+}_{R\,\mu}$$

Purely **right-handed** quark charged current. Colour is contracted diagonally
($c$ summed on both quarks), so $W_R$ is a colour singlet. The flavour mixing is
governed by the *separate* right-handed mixing matrix $V'_{\rm CKM}$ (`CKMR`),
not the SM CKM matrix.

`LWRQuarks`

$$\mathcal{L}_{W_Rq}
= \mathcal{L}_{W_Rq}^{\rm noHC} + \left(\mathcal{L}_{W_Rq}^{\rm noHC}\right)^{\dagger}
= -\,g_{W_R q}\sum_{i,j,c}\Big[
V^{\prime}_{ij}\,\bar u_{i}^{\,c}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,d_{j}^{\,c}\,W^{+}_{R\,\mu}
+ V^{\prime\,*}_{ij}\,\bar d_{j}^{\,c}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,u_{i}^{\,c}\,W^{-}_{R\,\mu}\Big]$$

`CKMR` is declared `ComplexParameter -> False`, so $V^{\prime *}_{ij}=V^{\prime}_{ij}$
and the h.c. term is simply the transposed-current version with $W_R^-$.

### 1.2 Charged current: $W_R$ with charged leptons and heavy neutrinos

`LWRLeptonsNonHC`

$$\mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell N}^{\rm noHC}
= -\,g_{W_R \ell}\sum_{\ell=1}^{3}\sum_{n=1}^{3}
Y_{\ell n}\;\bar N_{n}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,P_R\,\ell_{\ell}\;W^{+}_{R\,\mu}$$

The projector sits on the **charged lepton**: only $\ell_R$ participates. The
first index of $Y$ (`YR[ll,nn]`) is the charged-lepton flavour, the second is the
heavy-neutrino flavour.

### 1.3 Charged current: $W_R$ with charged leptons and *light* neutrinos

`LWRLightNuNonHC`

$$\mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell\nu}^{\rm noHC}
= -\,g_{W_R \ell}\sum_{\ell=1}^{3}\sum_{n=1}^{3}
X_{\ell n}\;\bar\nu_{n}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,P_R\,\ell_{\ell}\;W^{+}_{R\,\mu}$$

Same right-handed structure, but with the *light* SM neutrinos. In a pure
left-right model this coupling is absent; it is generated by light–heavy
neutrino mixing, and $X_{\ell\nu}$ (`XL`) is exactly that mixing (default value:
the zero matrix, i.e. switched off).

`LWRLeptons`

$$\mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell}
= \mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell N}^{\rm noHC} + \mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell\nu}^{\rm noHC} + \mathrm{h.c.}$$

$$= -\,g_{W_R \ell}\sum_{\ell,n}\Big[
Y_{\ell n}\,\bar N_{n}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,\ell_{\ell}\,W^{+}_{R\,\mu}
+ X_{\ell n}\,\bar\nu_{n}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,\ell_{\ell}\,W^{+}_{R\,\mu}
+ Y_{\ell n}\,\bar \ell_{\ell}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,N_{n}\,W^{-}_{R\,\mu}
+ X_{\ell n}\,\bar \ell_{\ell}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,\nu_{n}\,W^{-}_{R\,\mu}\Big]$$

using $(\bar\psi_1\gamma^\mu P_R\psi_2)^{\dagger}=\bar\psi_2\gamma^\mu P_R\psi_1$
and the fact that both `YR` and `XL` are real.

### 1.4 Neutral current: $Z_R$ with quarks

`LZRQuarks`

$$\mathcal{L}_{Z_Rq}
= g_{Z_R q}\sum_{i=1}^{3}\sum_{c=1}^{3}\Big[
\bar u_{i}^{\,c}\gamma^{\mu}\big(g^{u}_{L}P_L + g^{u}_{R}P_R\big)u_{i}^{\,c}
+ \bar d_{i}^{\,c}\gamma^{\mu}\big(g^{d}_{L}P_L + g^{d}_{R}P_R\big)d_{i}^{\,c}
\Big]\,Z_{R\,\mu}$$

Flavour-**diagonal** and generation-**universal** (one sum over $i$, same field
on both sides), colour diagonal. $g^{u}_{L}=$ `gZRuL`, etc.

### 1.5 Neutral current: $Z_R$ with leptons

`LZRLeptons`

$$\mathcal{L}_{Z_R\ell}
= g_{Z_R \ell}\sum_{i=1}^{3}\Big[
\bar \ell_{i}\gamma^{\mu}\big(g^{e}_{L}P_L + g^{e}_{R}P_R\big)\ell_{i}
+ g^{\nu}_{L}\,\bar \nu_{i}\gamma^{\mu}P_L\,\nu_{i}
+ g^{N}_{R}\,\bar N_{i}\gamma^{\mu}P_R\,N_{i}
\Big]\,Z_{R\,\mu}$$

Three points of detail:

* The light neutrinos couple **only** through $P_L$ (no right-handed light
  neutrino exists), with $g^{\nu}_{L}=$ `gZRnuL`.
* The heavy neutrinos couple **only** through $P_R$, with $g^{N}_{R}=\tfrac12$
  exactly (no $x$-dependence), because $N$ is a pure $SU(2)_R$ doublet member
  with $Q=0$.
* $N$ is Majorana, so $\bar N\gamma^\mu N\equiv 0$; the surviving current is
  purely axial:
  $\bar N_i\gamma^\mu P_R N_i = \tfrac12\,\bar N_i\gamma^\mu\gamma_5 N_i$.
  The $Z_R N N$ vertex is therefore an axial-vector vertex, with the usual
  Majorana factor-of-two combinatorics (`MajoranaPhase -> 1` is declared).

### 1.6 Total

`LBSM`

$$\mathcal{L}_{\rm BSM}
= \mathcal{L}_{W_Rq} + \mathcal{L}_{W_R\ell}
+ \mathcal{L}_{Z_Rq} + \mathcal{L}_{Z_R\ell}$$

Explicitly absent from $\mathcal{L}_{\rm BSM}$: kinetic terms, mass terms,
gauge self-interactions, any scalar (the file has no `S[...]` class), any
$W_R$–$W$ or $Z_R$–$Z$ mixing, and any $N$–$\nu$ Yukawa or mass-mixing term.

## 4. Physics summary

This is an **effective (simplified) left-right symmetric model**: a heavy
right-handed charged boson $W_R^\pm$ at 3 TeV, a heavy neutral boson $Z_R$ at
5.07 TeV, and three heavy **Majorana** neutrinos $N_{1,2,3}$, coupled to the
Standard Model through purely right-handed currents whose strength is set by two
free ratios $\kappa_R^{q}$ and $\kappa_R^{\ell}$ (quark and lepton sectors
decoupled), one right-handed quark mixing matrix $V'_{\rm CKM}$, and two
neutrino mixing matrices $Y_{\ell N}$ (heavy) and $X_{\ell\nu}$ (light). Only
interaction terms are written: there is no scalar sector, no $W_L$–$W_R$ mixing
and no gauge self-interactions, so the model is a phenomenological
parametrisation of the heavy-state couplings rather than a complete gauge theory.

The processes it mediates are the classic left-right collider signatures:
$s$-channel $q\bar q' \to W_R^\pm \to \ell^\pm N$ followed by
$N \to \ell^\pm q \bar q'$ through an off-shell $W_R$ — the Keung–Senjanović
same-sign dilepton plus jets final state, which is lepton-number violating
because $N$ is Majorana (`MajoranaPhase -> 1`), and whose lepton flavour
composition is controlled by $Y_{\ell N}$. Because $M_{N_1}=173.3$ GeV is far
below $M_{W_R}=3$ TeV, the $N_1$ decay products are strongly boosted and
collimated (a "neutrino jet"). In parallel, $Z_R$ gives a narrow dilepton and
dijet resonance at 5.07 TeV, and also $Z_R\to N N$ pair production through the
purely axial $\bar N\gamma^\mu\gamma_5 N$ vertex; a non-zero $X_{\ell\nu}$ opens
$W_R \to \ell \nu$ with a light neutrino, i.e. a right-handed lepton-plus-missing-
energy channel. $N_2$ and $N_3$ are set to $10^{12}$ GeV and are decoupled.
