# Reconstruction of `sanitized.fr`

## 0. Gauge structure and conventions

The file adds one abelian gauge factor to the Standard Model. The total gauge
group is

$$
SU(3)_C \times SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y \times U(1)_X ,
$$

with gauge field $Z'_\mu$ (`Zp`) and gauge coupling $g_X$ (`gX`).

The SM part of the Lagrangian is imported as `LSM`; the file adds only the new
pieces. The new $U(1)_X$ interaction is **not** written with a `DC[]` covariant
derivative for the SM fermions. It is written out by hand in `LZpF`. The
implied covariant derivative is

$$
D_\mu = \partial_\mu
- i g_s T^a G^a_\mu
- i g\, T^i W^i_\mu
- i g' Y B_\mu
- i g_X x_f Z'_\mu ,
$$

where $x_f$ is the $U(1)_X$ charge of the field $f$. No kinetic mixing term
$B^{\mu\nu} Z'_{\mu\nu}$ is declared, so $Z'$ is treated as a mass eigenstate
at the Lagrangian level.

The $U(1)_X$ charges are internal parameters built from two free numbers,
$x_H$ (`xH`) and $x_\Phi$ (`xPhi`):

$$
x_f = x_H\, Y_f + x_\Phi\, (B-L)_f .
$$

This reproduces every charge in the file exactly:

| `.fr` symbol | value | $Y$ | $B-L$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `xqL` | $x_H/6 + x_\Phi/3$ | $1/6$ | $1/3$ |
| `xuR` | $2x_H/3 + x_\Phi/3$ | $2/3$ | $1/3$ |
| `xdR` | $-x_H/3 + x_\Phi/3$ | $-1/3$ | $1/3$ |
| `xlL` | $-x_H/2 - x_\Phi$ | $-1/2$ | $-1$ |
| `xeR` | $-x_H - x_\Phi$ | $-1$ | $-1$ |
| `xnR` | $-x_\Phi$ | $0$ | $-1$ |
| `xHd` | $x_H/2$ | $1/2$ | $0$ |
| `xSc` | $2 x_\Phi$ | $0$ | $2$ |

So $x_H = 0$ gives pure $U(1)_{B-L}$, and $x_\Phi = 0$ gives $U(1)_Y$-like
charges. The charge set is anomaly free because it is a linear combination of
$Y$ and $B-L$, and $B-L$ is made anomaly free by the three right-handed
neutrinos.

The SM Higgs doublet $H$ carries $x_{H_d} = x_H/2$. A new complex scalar
$\Phi$ carries $x_\Phi^{\rm sc} = 2 x_\Phi$. Only the real part of $\Phi$
survives in the file, as the physical real scalar $H_X$:

$$
\Phi = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(v_\Phi + H_X\right)
\qquad (\text{unitary gauge; the Goldstone is eaten by } Z') .
$$

FeynRules idioms used below: `FS[Zp,mu,nu]` $= \partial_\mu Z'_\nu - \partial_\nu Z'_\mu$;
`Ga[mu]` $= \gamma^\mu$; `ProjP` $= P_R = \tfrac{1}{2}(1+\gamma_5)$;
`Eps[i,j]` $= \epsilon_{ij}$ (SU(2) antisymmetric tensor, $\epsilon_{12}=+1$);
`HC[...]` = hermitian conjugate; `del[X,mu]` $= \partial_\mu X$.

## 1. Lagrangian, term by term

### `LZpKin` — $Z'$ kinetic and mass term

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'\,\rm kin}
= -\frac{1}{4}\, Z'_{\mu\nu} Z'^{\mu\nu}
+ \frac{1}{2}\, M_{Z'}^2\, Z'_\mu Z'^\mu ,
\qquad
Z'_{\mu\nu} \equiv \partial_\mu Z'_\nu - \partial_\nu Z'_\mu .
$$

The mass is put in by hand (unitary gauge). It is the mass generated by the two
vacuum expectation values, see $M_{Z'}$ in Section 3.

### `LZpF` — $Z'$ coupling to fermions

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
= -\, g_X\, Z'_\mu \Big[
x_{q_L}\, \bar{Q}_L \gamma^\mu Q_L
+ x_{u_R}\, \bar{u}_R \gamma^\mu u_R
+ x_{d_R}\, \bar{d}_R \gamma^\mu d_R
+ x_{\ell_L}\, \bar{L}_L \gamma^\mu L_L
+ x_{e_R}\, \bar{e}_R \gamma^\mu e_R
+ x_{n_R}\, \bar{N}_R \gamma^\mu N_R
\Big]
$$

All generation indices are summed and the coupling is flavour diagonal and
flavour universal. `ExpandIndices[..., FlavorExpand -> {SU2D}]` opens the
$SU(2)_L$ doublets, and the replacement rules
$V_{ab} V^{*}_{ac} \to \delta_{bc}$, $V_{ba} V^{*}_{ca} \to \delta_{bc}$
remove the CKM matrix that the doublet expansion introduces. In component form,
with $i$ the generation index:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
= -\, g_X\, Z'_\mu \Big[
x_{q_L}\big(\bar{u}^i_L \gamma^\mu u^i_L + \bar{d}^i_L \gamma^\mu d^i_L\big)
+ x_{u_R}\, \bar{u}^i_R \gamma^\mu u^i_R
+ x_{d_R}\, \bar{d}^i_R \gamma^\mu d^i_R
$$
$$
+\; x_{\ell_L}\big(\bar{\nu}^i_L \gamma^\mu \nu^i_L + \bar{e}^i_L \gamma^\mu e^i_L\big)
+ x_{e_R}\, \bar{e}^i_R \gamma^\mu e^i_R
+ x_{n_R}\, \bar{N}^i \gamma^\mu P_R N^i
\Big] .
$$

The last piece uses $N_R = P_R N_X$, so
$\bar{N}_R \gamma^\mu N_R = \bar{N}_X \gamma^\mu P_R N_X$. Because $N_X$ is a
Majorana field, this current is purely right-chiral and is the $B-L$ current of
the heavy neutrinos.

### `LNXKin` — heavy neutrino kinetic and Majorana mass term

$$
\mathcal{L}_{N\,\rm kin}
= \frac{i}{2}\, \bar{N}_X^i \gamma^\mu \partial_\mu N_X^i
- \frac{1}{2}\, \bar{N}_X^i \,(m_N)_{ij}\, N_X^j ,
\qquad
(m_N)_{ij} = \mathrm{diag}\big(M_{N_1}, M_{N_2}, M_{N_3}\big) .
$$

The factors $1/2$ are the standard Majorana normalisation for a self-conjugate
field. The derivative is an ordinary derivative, because $N_X$ is an SM singlet
and its $U(1)_X$ current is already written in `LZpF`.

### `LHXKin` — new real scalar kinetic and mass term

$$
\mathcal{L}_{H_X\,\rm kin}
= \frac{1}{2}\, \partial_\mu H_X\, \partial^\mu H_X
- \frac{1}{2}\, M_{H_X}^2\, H_X^2 .
$$

### `LZpHX` — $Z'$–$H_X$ gauge interaction

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Z'H_X}
= g_X^2\, x_{\rm sc}^2 \left( v_\Phi H_X + \frac{1}{2} H_X^2 \right) Z'_\mu Z'^\mu ,
\qquad x_{\rm sc} = 2 x_\Phi .
$$

This is the remainder of $\left|D_\mu \Phi\right|^2$ after the $H_X$ kinetic
term and the $Z'$ mass term are split off. It gives the trilinear
$Z'Z'H_X$ vertex, with strength $2 g_X^2 x_{\rm sc}^2 v_\Phi$, and the
quartic $Z'Z'H_XH_X$ vertex.

### `LNYuk` — heavy neutrino Yukawa coupling to $H_X$

$$
\mathcal{L}_{N\,\rm Yuk}
= -\frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\, H_X\, \bar{N}_X^i \,(y_N)_{ij}\, N_X^j
= -\frac{1}{2}\sum_i \frac{M_{N_i}}{v_\Phi}\, H_X\, \bar{N}_X^i N_X^i ,
$$

using $(y_N)_{ij} = \sqrt{2}\, M_{N_i}\, \delta_{ij} / v_\Phi$. This is the
$H_X$ part of the Majorana mass operator
$-\tfrac{1}{2\sqrt2} y_N \Phi \overline{N^c} P_R N + \text{h.c.}$, whose vacuum
expectation value part is already the mass term in `LNXKin`.

### `LNuYuk` — Dirac neutrino Yukawa coupling

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu\,\rm Yuk}
= -\, (y_\nu)_{ij}\, \bar{L}_L^{\,a,i}\, N_R^{\,j}\, \widetilde{H}^{a}
\;+\; \mathrm{h.c.} ,
\qquad
\widetilde{H}^{a} \equiv \epsilon_{ab}\, H^{*b} = (i\sigma_2 H^*)^a .
$$

Written exactly as in the file, with $a,b$ the $SU(2)_L$ indices:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu\,\rm Yuk}
= -\, (y_\nu)_{ij}\, \epsilon_{ab}\, \bar{L}_L^{\,a,i}\, P_R N_X^{\,j}\, H^{*b}
\;-\; (y_\nu)^{*}_{ij}\, \epsilon_{ab}\, H^{b}\, \bar{N}_X^{\,j} P_L L_L^{\,a,i} .
$$

In components, after $H \to (0, (v+h)/\sqrt2)$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\nu\,\rm Yuk} \supset
-\frac{v+h}{\sqrt{2}} (y_\nu)_{ij}\, \bar{\nu}_L^{\,i} P_R N_X^{\,j}
+ \text{h.c.}
$$

The `feynmangaugerules` block sets the Goldstone fields $G^0, G^\pm$ to zero
when `FeynmanGauge` is `False`. It is a gauge bookkeeping device, not new
physics.

### `LTotal` — total Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal{L}
= \mathcal{L}_{\rm SM}
+ \mathcal{L}_{Z'\,\rm kin}
+ \mathcal{L}_{Z'F}
+ \mathcal{L}_{N\,\rm kin}
+ \mathcal{L}_{H_X\,\rm kin}
+ \mathcal{L}_{Z'H_X}
+ \mathcal{L}_{N\,\rm Yuk}
+ \mathcal{L}_{\nu\,\rm Yuk} .
$$

### Note on an absent term

The scalar sector is incomplete as written. The external parameter `lamHX`
($\lambda'$, the Higgs portal quartic $\lambda' |H|^2 |\Phi|^2$) is declared but
appears in **no** Lagrangian term and in **no** internal parameter. `lamX`
($\lambda_\Phi$) appears only inside $M_{H_X}$, never in a vertex, so the
cubic $\lambda_\Phi v_\Phi H_X^3$ and quartic $\lambda_\Phi H_X^4$ self
couplings are also absent. A complete model would contain a scalar potential
term of the form

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\rm pot} \supset
-\lambda_\Phi \left( |\Phi|^2 - \frac{v_\Phi^2}{2} \right)^2
- \lambda' \left( |H|^2 - \frac{v^2}{2} \right)\left( |\Phi|^2 - \frac{v_\Phi^2}{2} \right) ,
$$

which would give $H_X^3$, $H_X^4$, $h H_X$, $h^2 H_X$, $h H_X^2$ and
$h^2 H_X^2$ vertices and the $h$–$H_X$ mixing angle. The file as given has no
$h$–$H_X$ portal at all, so $H_X$ talks to the SM only through $Z'$ and through
the heavy neutrinos.

## 4. Physics summary

This is a general (non-universal) $U(1)_X$ extension of the Standard Model. The
charge of each SM field is the combination $x_f = x_H Y_f + x_\Phi (B-L)_f$, so
one model file covers $U(1)_{B-L}$, $U(1)_{R}$-like and all intermediate cases
by a change of two numbers. The new states are a heavy neutral gauge boson $Z'$
(7.5 TeV by default), a heavy real scalar $H_X$ (4.2 TeV) from the $U(1)_X$
breaking scalar $\Phi$, and three right-handed Majorana neutrinos $N_{X\,1,2,3}$
(8 TeV each) that make the charge set anomaly free.

The interactions are a flavour-universal, chirality-dependent $Z'$ current to
all SM fermions and to the heavy neutrinos; a $Z'Z'H_X$ and $Z'Z'H_XH_X$ gauge
vertex; a diagonal $H_X \bar{N}_X N_X$ Yukawa coupling fixed by
$M_{N_i}/v_\Phi$; and a tiny Dirac Yukawa coupling $y_\nu \sim 10^{-6}$ that
joins $\nu_L$ to $N_X$. Together the Dirac and Majorana terms are a type-I
seesaw, giving light neutrino masses $m_\nu \simeq y_\nu^2 v^2 / (2 M_N)$.

Such a model mediates Drell-Yan production $pp \to Z' \to \ell^+\ell^-,\;
q\bar{q},\; \nu\bar\nu$, which is the main collider signature; pair production
of heavy neutrinos $pp \to Z' \to N_X N_X$ followed by
$N_X \to \ell^\pm W^\mp,\; \nu Z,\; \nu h$, which gives same-sign dilepton
final states that violate lepton number by two units; and $Z' \to Z' H_X$-type
cascades plus $H_X \to N_X N_X$ and $H_X \to Z' Z'$ decays. Note that, as
written, the file has no Higgs portal term, so $H_X$ does not mix with the SM
Higgs and cannot be produced through it.
