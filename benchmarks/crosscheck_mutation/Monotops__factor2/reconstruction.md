# Reconstruction of `sanitized.fr`

## 0. Conventions used below

- Generation (flavour) indices `ff, ff1, ff2, ff3` are written $f, f_1, f_2, f_3 = 1,2,3$; colour (fundamental SU(3)$_C$) indices `cc, cc1, cc2, cc3` are written $i,j,k = 1,2,3$. All repeated indices are summed.
- `uq[f,i]` $= u_{f}^{i}$ is the SM up-type quark triplet $(u,c,t)$, `dq[f,i]` $= d_{f}^{i}$ the down-type triplet $(d,s,b)$. `uqbar` / `dqbar` / `chibar` are Dirac adjoints $\bar u$, $\bar d$, $\bar\chi$.
- `Ga[mu]` $=\gamma^\mu$, `Ga[5]` $=\gamma^5$. There are no `ProjP`/`ProjM` in the file: every fermion bilinear appears in the pair (scalar, pseudoscalar) $\{1,\gamma^5\}$ or (vector, axial-vector) $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\}$, so chirality is carried by the relative size of the `a`-type and `b`-type coefficients, not by explicit projectors. In chiral language $a\,\bar\psi_1\psi_2 + b\,\bar\psi_1\gamma^5\psi_2 = (a-b)\,\bar\psi_1 P_L\psi_2 + (a+b)\,\bar\psi_1 P_R\psi_2$, and likewise $a\,\bar\psi_1\gamma^\mu\psi_2 + b\,\bar\psi_1\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi_2 = (a-b)\,\bar\psi_1\gamma^\mu P_L\psi_2 + (a+b)\,\bar\psi_1\gamma^\mu P_R\psi_2$.
- `CC[psi]` is the charge-conjugate field $\psi^{c} = C\bar\psi^{T}$, so `bar[CC[psi]]` $= \overline{\psi^{c}} = -\psi^{T}C^{-1}$. Below it is written $\overline{d^{c}}$; the explicit form is $\overline{d^{c}_{f_2}}\,\Gamma\, q_{f_3} = -d_{f_2}^{T}C^{-1}\Gamma\,q_{f_3}$.
- `HC[term]` is the hermitian conjugate, written $+\,\mathrm{h.c.}$
- `Eps[i,j,k]` $=\epsilon^{ijk}$, the totally antisymmetric SU(3)$_C$ invariant tensor. $\epsilon^{ijk}\Phi^{i}q^{j}q^{k}$ contracts $\mathbf{3}\otimes\mathbf{3}\otimes\mathbf{3}\to\mathbf{1}$.
- `FS[A,mu,nu]` for a field that is not a declared gauge boson is the abelian field strength $\partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu$.

**Covariant derivative.** The file declares no new gauge group, so `DC` is the SM covariant derivative acting on whatever unbroken charges the field carries. The new fields carry only colour (an `Index[Colour]`, i.e. the fundamental $\mathbf{3}$) and the electric charge `Q`; none of them is given an SU(2)$_L$ index or a hypercharge, so they are electroweak singlets and only gluons and the photon appear:

$$
D_\mu \Phi^{i} \;=\; \Big(\partial_\mu\,\delta^{ij} \;-\; i\,g_s\,G_\mu^{a}\,T^{a\,ij} \;-\; i\,e\,Q_\Phi\,A_\mu\,\delta^{ij}\Big)\Phi^{j},
\qquad T^{a}=\tfrac{\lambda^{a}}{2}.
$$

For the colour- and charge-neutral Dirac fermion $\chi$, `DC` degenerates to the ordinary derivative, $D_\mu\chi=\partial_\mu\chi$.

## 1. Lagrangian, term by term

### Kinetic and mass terms

**`Lphikin`** — real (self-conjugate) scalar singlet:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\phi,\text{kin}} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi \;-\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,M_\phi^{2}\,\phi^{2}
$$

**`LVkin`** — real (self-conjugate) massive vector singlet, Proca form, $V_{\mu\nu}\equiv\partial_\mu V_\nu-\partial_\nu V_\mu$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{V,\text{kin}} \;=\; -\tfrac{1}{4}\,V_{\mu\nu}V^{\mu\nu} \;+\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,M_V^{2}\,V_\mu V^{\mu}
$$

**`Lchikin`** — Dirac fermion singlet (gauge singlet, so $D_\mu\to\partial_\mu$):

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\chi,\text{kin}} \;=\; i\,\bar\chi\,\gamma^{\mu}\,\partial_\mu\chi \;-\; M_\chi\,\bar\chi\chi
$$

**`Lvphikin`** — complex colour-triplet scalar, $Q_\varphi=+2/3$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\varphi,\text{kin}} \;=\; \big(D_\mu\varphi\big)^{\dagger i}\big(D^{\mu}\varphi\big)^{i} \;-\; M_\varphi^{2}\,\varphi^{\dagger i}\varphi^{i},
\qquad D_\mu\varphi^{i}=\big(\partial_\mu\delta^{ij}-ig_sG^a_\mu T^{a\,ij}-\tfrac{2}{3}\,i\,e\,A_\mu\delta^{ij}\big)\varphi^{j}
$$

**`Lvphitkin`** — complex colour-triplet scalar, $Q_{\tilde\varphi}=-1/3$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi,\text{kin}} \;=\; \big(D_\mu\tilde\varphi\big)^{\dagger i}\big(D^{\mu}\tilde\varphi\big)^{i} \;-\; M_{\tilde\varphi}^{2}\,\tilde\varphi^{\dagger i}\tilde\varphi^{i},
\qquad D_\mu\tilde\varphi^{i}=\big(\partial_\mu\delta^{ij}-ig_sG^a_\mu T^{a\,ij}+\tfrac{1}{3}\,i\,e\,A_\mu\delta^{ij}\big)\tilde\varphi^{j}
$$

**`LXkin`** — complex colour-triplet massive vector, $Q_X=+2/3$. The file uses `FS`, not `DC`, so the field strength written here is the **abelian** one, $X^{i}_{\mu\nu}\equiv\partial_\mu X^{i}_\nu-\partial_\nu X^{i}_\mu$ (the colour index is a spectator; no gluon or photon term is generated by this kinetic term):

$$
\mathcal{L}_{X,\text{kin}} \;=\; -\tfrac{1}{2}\,X^{\dagger i}_{\mu\nu}X^{i\,\mu\nu} \;+\; M_X^{2}\,X^{\dagger i}_{\mu}X^{i\,\mu}
$$

### Neutral-mediator flavour-changing couplings to up-type quarks

**`Lphiu`** — scalar/pseudoscalar coupling of the real singlet $\phi$ to up-type quarks. The file carries an explicit overall factor of **2** in front of this term (and no such factor in `LVu`); it is reproduced literally:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\phi u} \;=\; 2\,\phi\;\sum_{f_1,f_2}\Big[\,a^{0}_{f_1f_2}\;\bar u_{f_1}^{\,i}\,u_{f_2}^{\,i} \;+\; b^{0}_{f_1f_2}\;\bar u_{f_1}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{5}\,u_{f_2}^{\,i}\,\Big]
$$

Colour is contracted flavour-diagonally on the quark line ($i$ summed), so this is a colour singlet. No `HC` is applied; hermiticity requires $a^{0}$ hermitian and $b^{0}$ anti-hermitian, which the real symmetric default values satisfy for $a^0$ ($b^0=0$).

**`LVu`** — vector/axial coupling of the real singlet vector $V$ to up-type quarks:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{Vu} \;=\; V_\mu\;\sum_{f_1,f_2}\Big[\,a^{1}_{f_1f_2}\;\bar u_{f_1}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,u_{f_2}^{\,i} \;+\; b^{1}_{f_1f_2}\;\bar u_{f_1}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{5}\,u_{f_2}^{\,i}\,\Big]
$$

### Coloured scalar $\varphi$ ($Q=+2/3$)

**`LvphiDiquark`** — antisymmetric (in colour) di-quark coupling to two down-type quarks:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\varphi\,qq} \;=\; \epsilon^{ijk}\,\varphi^{i}\sum_{f_2,f_3}\Big[\,a^{q}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,d_{f_3}^{\,k} \;+\; b^{q}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,\gamma^{5}\,d_{f_3}^{\,k}\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

Because $\epsilon^{ijk}$ is antisymmetric in $j\leftrightarrow k$ and the bilinears $\overline{\psi^{c}}\chi$, $\overline{\psi^{c}}\gamma^{5}\chi$ are symmetric under exchange of the two fermions, only the **flavour-antisymmetric** part of $a^{q},b^{q}$ contributes — consistent with the default values $a^{q}_{12}=+0.1$, $a^{q}_{21}=-0.1$.

**`LvphiChi`** — coupling of $\varphi$ to an up-type quark and the singlet fermion $\chi$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\varphi\chi} \;=\; \varphi^{i}\sum_{f}\Big[\,a^{1/2}_{f}\;\bar u_{f}^{\,i}\,\chi \;+\; b^{1/2}_{f}\;\bar u_{f}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{5}\,\chi\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

Charge balance: $Q_\varphi - Q_u + Q_\chi = 2/3 - 2/3 + 0 = 0$; colour: $\mathbf{3}\otimes\bar{\mathbf{3}}\to\mathbf{1}$.

### Coloured scalar $\tilde\varphi$ ($Q=-1/3$)

**`LvphitDiquark`** — di-quark coupling to one down-type and one up-type quark:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi\,qq} \;=\; \epsilon^{ijk}\,\tilde\varphi^{i}\sum_{f_2,f_3}\Big[\,\tilde a^{q}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,u_{f_3}^{\,k} \;+\; \tilde b^{q}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,\gamma^{5}\,u_{f_3}^{\,k}\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

Here the two quarks are of different type, so no flavour (anti)symmetry condition applies.

**`LvphitChi`** — coupling of $\tilde\varphi$ to a down-type quark and $\chi$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi\chi} \;=\; \tilde\varphi^{i}\sum_{f}\Big[\,\tilde a^{1/2}_{f}\;\bar d_{f}^{\,i}\,\chi \;+\; \tilde b^{1/2}_{f}\;\bar d_{f}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{5}\,\chi\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

### Coloured vector $X$ ($Q=+2/3$)

**`LXDiquark`** — vector/axial di-quark coupling to two down-type quarks:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{X\,qq} \;=\; \epsilon^{ijk}\,X^{i}_{\mu}\sum_{f_2,f_3}\Big[\,a^{q,V}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,d_{f_3}^{\,k} \;+\; b^{q,V}_{f_2f_3}\;\overline{d^{\,c}_{f_2}}^{\,j}\,\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{5}\,d_{f_3}^{\,k}\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

For the vector current $\overline{\psi^{c}}\gamma^{\mu}\chi$ the fermion-exchange symmetry is opposite to the scalar case, so with the antisymmetric $\epsilon^{ijk}$ only the **flavour-symmetric** part of $a^{q,V}$ survives (and the antisymmetric part of $b^{q,V}$) — consistent with the default diagonal value $a^{q,V}_{11}=0.1$.

**`LXChi`** — coupling of $X$ to an up-type quark and $\chi$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{X\chi} \;=\; X^{i}_{\mu}\sum_{f}\Big[\,a^{1/2,V}_{f}\;\bar u_{f}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{\mu}\,\chi \;+\; b^{1/2,V}_{f}\;\bar u_{f}^{\,i}\,\gamma^{\mu}\gamma^{5}\,\chi\Big] \;+\; \mathrm{h.c.}
$$

### Total

**`LTotal`**

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\phi,\text{kin}}+\mathcal{L}_{V,\text{kin}}+\mathcal{L}_{\chi,\text{kin}}+\mathcal{L}_{\varphi,\text{kin}}+\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi,\text{kin}}+\mathcal{L}_{X,\text{kin}}
+\mathcal{L}_{\phi u}+\mathcal{L}_{Vu}+\mathcal{L}_{\varphi qq}+\mathcal{L}_{\varphi\chi}+\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi qq}+\mathcal{L}_{\tilde\varphi\chi}+\mathcal{L}_{Xqq}+\mathcal{L}_{X\chi}
$$

## 4. Physics summary

This is a monotop-style dark-matter / baryon-number-violating simplified model: it adds one gauge-singlet Dirac fermion $\chi$ (invisible, 50 GeV, zero width), a neutral singlet scalar $\phi$ and a neutral singlet vector $V$ with flavour-changing $u$–$t$ couplings, plus three coloured $\mathbf{3}$ mediators — a $Q=+2/3$ scalar $\varphi$, a $Q=-1/3$ scalar $\tilde\varphi$ and a $Q=+2/3$ vector $X$ — each of which has both a colour-antisymmetric di-quark vertex (violating baryon number by one unit) and a vertex joining one quark to $\chi$.

The two generic production paths are exactly the two monotop topologies. In the *flavour-changing neutral* channel, $g\,u \to t\,\phi$ or $g\,u\to t\,V$ (equivalently $u\bar u\to t\bar t$-type FCNC vertices) gives a single top recoiling against an invisible or long-lived neutral state. In the *resonant* channel, two down-type quarks fuse into the coloured mediator, $d\,s \to \varphi$, $d\,t\to\tilde\varphi$, $d\,d\to X$, which then decays through its quark–$\chi$ vertex to a single (top or light) quark plus the invisible $\chi$, e.g. $d s\to\varphi\to t\,\bar\chi$ and $d d \to X\to t\,\bar\chi$ with the chosen defaults.

The observable signature is therefore a single hard top quark plus large missing transverse energy (a "monotop"), together with the resonant di-quark decays back to jets ($\varphi,\tilde\varphi,X \to qq$) that give same-sign-quark / di-jet resonance and baryon-number-violating final states. Two features of the file as written are worth flagging for a checker: `Lphiu` carries an explicit overall factor of 2 that `LVu` does not, so the $\phi\bar uu$ Feynman rule is twice the naive $a^0$ normalization; and `LXkin` uses the abelian `FS` rather than `DC`, so the coloured vector's kinetic term as written contains no QCD covariantization.
