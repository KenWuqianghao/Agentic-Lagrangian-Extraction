"The model is real": the chain passes, no statement is cut, and every new particle is in at least one UFO vertex. This page has two kinds of example: runs that fail the chain, and runs that pass with a hollow or incomplete model.
A cut statement is the most common hidden defect in the three groups. In a .fr file a top-level statement that is a complete expression at the end of a line ends there; a next line that starts with + or - is a separate statement with no effect. FeynRules, the four checks and MadGraph do not report it.
