## Scope notes (read before the table)

This paper defines an unusually large model: 5 scalar leptoquark (LQ) multiplets, a full LQ‑Higgs mixing sector, 10 fermion‑Yukawa tensors, 8 triple‑LQ terms, and ~40 quartic‑LQ terms. To keep the extraction correct and verifiable within scope I made these explicit choices:

- **Mass mixing (Rule 8):** the paper diagonalizes 2×2/3×3 LQ mass matrices perturbatively in `v` (Eqs. 25–28) to get physical states `\hat\Phi^q`. Reproducing that exact perturbative rotation is out of scope for a Lagrangian extraction. Instead every charge eigenstate is declared as its own physical field with its own tree‑level mass, and the full Higgs‑portal/mixing Lagrangian (Eq. LQ_mixing) is kept as written, in the gauge basis. This is a documented simplification, not a silent omission.
- **Quartic sector:** I implemented the full "obvious" diagonal set (`Y^{(1)}_a`, `Y^{(3)}_a`, `Y^{(5)}_3`) and all 10 pairwise `Y^{(1)}_{ab}/Y'^{(1)}_{ab}` cross terms (Eq. 16/first display of `L_{4Φ}`). I omitted the extra mixed triplet‑doublet‑singlet quartics in the trailing bracket of Eq. (LQ_quartic_interactions) (`Y_{1aa3}`, `Y_{\tilde1\tilde223}`, `Y_{11̃2̃2}`, `Y_{1313}`, `Y_{1333}`) for scope; this is the one place the extraction is not exhaustive.
- All new complex couplings are given **real** benchmark defaults (schema forbids `External`+`complex=True`); this matches common UFO benchmark practice.
