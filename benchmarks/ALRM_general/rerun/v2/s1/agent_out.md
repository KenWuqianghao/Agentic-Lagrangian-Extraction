## Framing note

ALRSM is really a *full* gauge model (SU(3)c × SU(2)L × SU(2)R′ × U(1)B−L + global U(1)S). The harness declares SM.fr as the base file, so I follow the schema rule `gauge_groups = []` and instead (a) declare every new mass eigenstate and every new gauge-eigenstate field, and (b) write the new gauge currents (W′, Z′) **explicitly** rather than through `DC[]`. The U(1)B−L and U(1)S charges are carried as `QuantumNumbers` `BL` and `SS` on the unphysical chiral/scalar classes only (rule 5).
